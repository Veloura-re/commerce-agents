### Deep Dive into Archetype Mechanics

#### 1. The MEV / Arbitrageur Archetype (`axiom`, `arbitrager`)
* **Behavior:** Identifies price discrepancies between Pump.fun, Raydium CPMM, Raydium CLMM, and Orca pools.
* **Execution Pattern:** Emits multi-leg atomic instructions in a single transaction (e.g., Buy on Pool A and Sell on Pool B within the exact same slot).
* **Why Copying Fails:** A copy bot only parses and duplicates the buy leg. The arbitrageur closes their sell leg in the same block, leaving the copier holding an unhedged, illiquid asset without arbitrage protection.

#### 2. The High-Speed Sniper Archetype (`trojan`, `photon`)
* **Behavior:** Submits transactions directly to validator block-engines (Jito) targeting block 0 or block 1 of pool initialization or migration.
* **Execution Pattern:** Micro-entry sizing (0.5 to 2 SOL), ultra-high frequency (200 to 500 trades/day), and aggressive take-profit dumping as soon as public buyers enter.
* **Why Copying Fails:** 
  $$\text{Copier PnL} = \text{Target PnL} - \text{Latency Drift (1-3s)} - \text{Slippage (10-25%)} - \text{Priority Fees}$$
  Because snipers enter when liquidity depth is under $20k, a 2-second copy delay forces the copier to buy at a 30% to 100% price premium, serving as the sniper's exit fill.

#### 3. The KOL / Wash-Trader Archetype (`wash_trader`, `top_followed`)
* **Behavior:** High social following (10k-50k followers). Emits rapid buy/sell cycles to trigger GMGN "Trending" and "Top Buyer" volume rankings.
* **Execution Pattern:** As observed on-chain with tracked KOL `Putrickk`, positions of 14.9M tokens were bought at timestamp `1789996039` and liquidated at timestamp `1789996041` (a 2-second hold time).
* **Why Copying Fails:** Downstream followers and copy bots absorb the sell orders generated by the influencer within seconds of publication.

#### 4. The Token Factory Developer Archetype (`created_token_count > 20`)
* **Behavior:** Wallets that launch dozens or hundreds of tokens via launchpad curves (e.g., Pump.fun).
* **Execution Pattern:** As documented in wallet `ohzarke` (`created_token_count = 111`), the wallet holds seed allocations or bundles snipes on its own deployments.
* **Why Copying Fails:** Entry metrics and win rates are artificial because the creator controls the initial liquidity and token distribution.

#### 5. The Fundamental Smart Money Whale Archetype (`smart_degen`)
* **Behavior:** Enters established tokens after initial price discovery (market cap > $300,000 to $1,000,000). Holds positions for hours to days.
* **Execution Pattern:** Position sizes between 5 SOL and 50 SOL. Sells into strength in staggered blocks.
* **Why Copying Succeeds:** Deep pool liquidity reduces slippage to < 3%. A 2-to-5-second execution delay results in less than 1% price drift, allowing the copier to mirror the underlying edge.

## 5. Algorithmic Risk Management Engine: "Sell Inits" & Trailing Exits

A trading bot must never depend on the target wallet for its exit strategy. If the target wallet is compromised, blacklisted, or abandons the token, the copier must exit systematically.

### 1. The "Sell Inits" Capital Recovery Algorithm
* **Objective:** Remove 100% of the invested SOL principal as soon as the position achieves sufficient unrealized gain, leaving remaining tokens as risk-free exposure.
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{Net Unrecovered Cost Basis } (\Delta C) = \sum C_{\text{in}} - \sum R_{\text{out}}$$
  $$\text{Tokens to Liquidate } (T_{\text{sell}}) = \frac{\Delta C}{P_{\text{bid}} \times (1 - \text{Slippage})}$$
* **Execution Trigger:** When current holding valuation exceeds $1.5 \times \Delta C$, submit a limit sell for $T_{\text{sell}}$.

### 2. Multi-Tier Take-Profit Schedule
* **Tier 1:** Sell 35% of position at $+100\%$ ($2.0\times$ entry price).
* **Tier 2:** Sell 35% of initial balance at $+300\%$ ($4.0\times$ entry price).
* **Tier 3 (Moonbag):** Activate Trailing Stop-Loss on remaining 30%.

### 3. Trailing Stop-Loss Formula
* Continuously track peak price $P_{\text{peak}} = \max(P_{\text{peak}}, P_{\text{current}})$.
* When $P_{\text{peak}} \ge 1.5 \times P_{\text{entry}}$, set dynamic stop threshold:
  $$P_{\text{stop}} = P_{\text{peak}} \times (1 - \text{Drawdown Tolerance})$$
* If $P_{\text{current}} \le P_{\text{stop}}$, execute immediate market exit.
